# How Expert Roundups Drive Authority Without Original Writing

A short note on one of the most reliable curation formats in the
no-original-writing playbook.

## What is an Expert Roundup?

An Expert Roundup is a curated quote-collection where a publisher
sources short opinions from N domain experts on a single tightly-scoped
question, then publishes the result with attribution. The publisher
adds value through (a) selecting good experts, (b) framing the
question, and (c) editing the responses into a coherent piece — but
writes none of the actual content.

## Why it works

Expert Roundups stack three different forms of authority in one
artifact:

1. The original experts lend their credibility to the piece.
2. The curator demonstrates judgement by choosing them.
3. The aggregate exceeds what any single expert contributes alone.

This makes the Roundup format particularly powerful for new
publications building authority in a niche — you don't need to be the
expert; you need to know who is.
